{{- if eq .Values.service.type "LoadBalancer" }}

Docker Desktop maps LoadBalancer services to localhost, so once EXTERNAL-IP
shows "localhost" (may take a few seconds):
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get svc {{ .Release.Name }}

Open the console (self-signed cert — your browser will warn, accept it):
  https://localhost:{{ .Values.service.ports.consoleGateway }}/ui/directory
{{- else }}

Port-forward to reach it locally:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ .Release.Name }} {{ .Values.service.ports.consoleGateway }}:{{ .Values.service.ports.consoleGateway }}

Then open (self-signed cert — your browser will warn, accept it):
  https://localhost:{{ .Values.service.ports.consoleGateway }}/ui/directory
{{- end }}
